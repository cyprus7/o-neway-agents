Map facts are community observations, not verified hotel conditions, live room availability or prices. Way coordinates are bounding-box midpoints. The slice includes hotel POIs within 8km of the documented Larnaca and Paphos centers, including nearby settlements, and is not complete inventory. Object edit timestamps describe OSM edits, not property verification dates.
